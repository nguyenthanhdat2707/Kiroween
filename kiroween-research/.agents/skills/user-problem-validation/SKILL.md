# User problem validation

Identify a specific present-day user and job, current alternative, remaining friction and evidence of demand. Distinguish direct user evidence from inference and assumed demand. Seek opposing evidence that modern replacements already solve the job. Record the smallest interview or observation that could falsify the need. Do not invent user interviews or infer a market from historical adoption.
