# Idea red team

For each candidate, find its strongest direct or adjacent product, open-source work and prior hackathon analogue. Compare the central job, not feature-count trivia. Apply five gates: authentic obsolete technology, present need, meaningful difference, achievable core demo, verified contest fit. State the strongest objection, evidence gap and a falsifier. Use REJECTED, NEEDS EVIDENCE, PROMISING or STRONG CANDIDATE. Do not rescue a failed idea by appending features.
