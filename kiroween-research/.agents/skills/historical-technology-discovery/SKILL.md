# Historical technology discovery

Build a record with period, original function and users, evidence of adoption, reason for decline, successor, current status, gains from replacement, possibly lost value, and direct source URLs. Separate discontinued products from niche but active technologies. Prefer archives, standards bodies, museums, creators and contemporary records. Mark unsupported timeline or causal claims as unknown. Only then propose a present-day use case.
